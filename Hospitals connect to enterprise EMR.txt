Hospitals connect to enterprise EMR/EHR systems through a combination of secure networks, interoperability standards, APIs, and health information exchanges. Enterprise EHRs are designed to integrate many departments, facilities, and external healthcare organizations into a single ecosystem.

1. Internal Hospital Integration

Within a hospital, different systems connect to the EHR:

Patient Registration
Appointment Scheduling
Laboratory Information System (LIS)
Pharmacy Management System
Radiology Information System (RIS)
Billing and Claims
Inventory Management
Bed Management

All these modules exchange data through APIs or healthcare messaging standards.

2. Healthcare Interoperability Standards

Most enterprise EHRs use standards such as:

HL7
The most widely used healthcare messaging standard.
Used to exchange patient information, lab orders, admissions, and discharge records.
FHIR
Modern REST API-based standard.
Uses JSON and HTTPS.
Easier to integrate with web and mobile applications.

Example:

GET /Patient/123
Authorization: Bearer token

Returns patient data in JSON format.

DICOM
Used for medical imaging.
Connects MRI, CT Scan, X-Ray, and Ultrasound systems.
3. Enterprise EHR Platforms

Large healthcare organizations often use systems such as:

Epic Systems
Oracle Health (formerly Cerner)
MEDITECH
athenahealth
eClinicalWorks

Hospitals connect to these platforms through:

Secure VPN connections
Dedicated healthcare networks
Cloud-hosted APIs
HL7/FHIR integration engines
4. Health Information Exchanges (HIE)

An HIE allows different hospitals and clinics to share patient information.

Example:

Patient visits Hospital A.
Records are stored in the EHR.
Patient visits Hospital B.
Hospital B retrieves records through the HIE.
Doctors can view previous diagnoses, medications, and lab results.
5. Integration Engines

Enterprise environments often use middleware such as:

Mirth Connect (NextGen Connect)
InterSystems HealthShare
Rhapsody Integration Engine

These platforms:

Transform HL7 messages
Route data between systems
Validate incoming information
Handle thousands of transactions daily
